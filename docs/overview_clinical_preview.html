Columns: results entering the unsuppressed register, dated by result received at facility. Line: share of all viral load results received that month that were suppressed (<1,000 c/mL), among active clients.

Women 2,291 (67.0%) Men 1,128 (33.0%) Suppression rate — solid where reliable 12-month suppression 97.6% · 85,068 results
Why the line only firms up from April. The treatment line list carries one viral load per client — their latest — so a client retested in March no longer shows the result they had in September. Checking the register's known unsuppressed episodes against the current list: 17% of August 2025's survive, against 98% of May 2026's. Older months are not wrong so much as hollowed out, and hollowed out unevenly — unsuppressed clients retest soonest, so they vanish from old months first and leave a residue that looks better than it was. The rate is drawn solid only where the snapshot still holds 94%+ of the month.
The 97.6% twelve-month figure is sound — one current result per client is exactly the right basis for it. The faint months are not evidence yet, but they become evidence: each weekly upload records its own recent months reliably, so a true series builds forward from here. Columns come from the register, which never loses an episode, and are complete except July 2026, where cover stops at 3 July. Suppression is among active clients with a numeric VL and a result date (85,068 of 87,386), threshold <1,000 c/mL.
