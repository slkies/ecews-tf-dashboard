Programme narrative · FY26 to date

Counselling is working. Retesting is the bottleneck.

2,597 treatment-failure episodes were recorded across Delta, Osun and Ekiti in FY26 — affecting 2,447 clients, of whom 150 failed more than once. Delta carries 66% of the burden.

Where a repeat viral load is done, the result is strong: 92.1% re-suppress below 1,000 copies/ml, and 275 of 342 become fully undetectable. Adherence counselling, when it completes and is followed by a test, works.

The programme loses the cohort twice before reaching that point. 483 episodes never commenced EAC at all — a third of the cohort. And of the 1,749 that did, only 19.6% have a post-EAC viral load result. 1,407 counselled episodes are still waiting for a repeat test. That queue, not counselling quality, is the binding constraint in FY26.

Beneath the aggregate, two groups fail differently: repeat-failure episodes are 3× less likely to re-suppress (OR 0.31), as are clients already on second-line. Both belong in front of a switch committee sooner than the cascade currently moves them.
